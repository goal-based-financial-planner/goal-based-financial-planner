Goal Based Financial Planner

A free, privacy-friendly financial planning tool. Set financial goals such as retirement, education, or buying a home; choose how to allocate investments across mutual funds, stocks, fixed deposits, and other options; and see the monthly SIP amount needed to reach each goal on time — adjusted for inflation.

  • Goal-based planning with target amounts and timelines
  • Automatic investment allocation and required-SIP calculations
  • Portfolio growth projections and visual progress tracking
  • Scenario comparison and per-goal risk indicators
  • Your data stays with you — saved to a local file or your own Google Drive
